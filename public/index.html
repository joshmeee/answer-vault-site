AnswerVault

Save reusable answers. Fill long business forms faster.

AnswerVault is a Chrome extension for people who answer the same questions across RFPs, vendor questionnaires, security reviews, grants, and procurement portals.

It stores your answers locally in the browser, scans visible form fields on the page you're already on, and fills the right answer only when you click. No auto-submit. No remote AI. No upload of your saved answer content.

How it works

  1. Save reusable answers, tagged by topic.
  2. Open the AnswerVault popup on any business form.
  3. Pick the right answer for each field and click fill.
  4. Review and submit the form yourself.
