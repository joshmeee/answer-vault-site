AnswerVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

AnswerVault is a local-first browser extension for saving reusable answers and filling long business forms faster.

Data Stored

AnswerVault stores the answers you create, including answer title, tags, body, and last updated timestamp. This data is stored locally using browser extension storage.

Data Transmission

The MVP does not send your answers to an external server. The MVP does not use remote AI APIs. The MVP does not sell, rent, or share your answer data.

Page Access

AnswerVault scans visible form fields on the active page after you open and use the extension so it can show fillable fields in the popup. Field scanning is used to support the form-fill workflow.

AnswerVault fills fields only after you click a fill button. AnswerVault does not submit forms for you.

Permissions

  • storage: stores reusable answers locally.
  • activeTab: lets the extension interact with the active tab when you use it.
  • scripting: scans and fills fields on the active tab only when you use the extension.

Business forms can appear on many different domains, including procurement portals, grant systems, vendor onboarding systems, and customer questionnaires. AnswerVault uses active-tab access for the form-fill workflow instead of running continuously across every page.
